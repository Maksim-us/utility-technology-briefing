Privacy Policy

Personal Health Analytics is a private application used solely by its owner for personal health, fitness, sleep, recovery, and activity analysis.

Google user data accessed

With explicit authorization, the application may access Google Health information including activity and fitness data, sleep data, and health metrics and measurements.

How data is used

The data is used only to create personal health summaries, analyze trends, evaluate recovery and activity patterns, and support personal fitness coaching and planning.

Storage

Health information may be stored on private infrastructure controlled by the application owner and in private Google Drive storage used by the personal analytics workflow.

Sharing and sale of data

Google user data accessed by this application is not sold, rented, advertised against, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is used only as part of the owner's private personal analytics workflow.

Data retention and deletion

Stored data may be retained for longitudinal personal health analysis. The owner may delete locally stored data and associated exported files at any time.

Revoking access

Access to Google account data can be revoked at any time through the security and connected-app settings of the associated Google account.

Medical disclaimer

This application is intended for personal informational and fitness purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
