Personal Health Analytics

Personal Health Analytics is a private, personal-use application that retrieves health and fitness information from Google Health for analysis, trend review, recovery monitoring, and personal fitness coaching.

The application may access activity and fitness data, sleep data, and health measurements that I have authorized through my Google account.

The information is used only for my personal health analytics workflow. It is not offered as a public service and is not intended for medical diagnosis or treatment.

Data use

Authorized Google Health data may be stored within my private systems and private Google Drive storage so that it can be analyzed over time. The data is not sold or used for advertising.
